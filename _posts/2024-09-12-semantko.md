# Semantko: Game of Context & Semantic Connections

Semantko is a daily Slovenian word puzzle where you uncover the secret word by exploring semantic connections between concepts. Powered by AI and the extensive Slovenian PojemNet network (50,000+ concepts, 500,000+ links), Semantko offers a unique challenge in discovering hidden relationships. Find the links and solve the mystery word.

Available at [semantko.si](https://semantko.si/).
