# Timber AI: Digital Twin Against Deforestation

Digital twin platform for real-time environmental monitoring, with a focus on detecting deforestation using satellite data and AI. Integrates segmentation models and leverages Sentinel Hub API for accessing Sentinel-2 imagery. The platform supports easy integration of new models and helps build custom datasets, contributing to research on many remote sensing fields. Made as part of [Vseslovenski Arnes HackathON 2025](https://hackathon.si/resitve-ekip-2025/).
